# Pion Contributor Licence Agreement

Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor Licence
Agreement v2.0, which is the text most contributors have already met.

## Why this exists, in two sentences

Pion is licensed under Apache-2.0, and its tuned vector kernels ship as a separately licensed binary library (the map is in `doc/licensing.md`). One CLA covers contributions to any part of the repository.
Keeping the option to relicense future versions, or to license the project
commercially, is only possible if one party holds sufficient rights to do
it, which is what this agreement establishes — it does **not** take your
copyright away.

## What you keep

**You keep the copyright in everything you write.** You are granting a
licence, not assigning ownership. You remain free to use, publish, relicense
or sell your own contribution however you like, including in other projects
under other licences.

## Entities

If you are contributing on behalf of a company rather than personally, say so
on the pull request. An entity agreement can be signed once and cover everyone
at that organisation; contact the Maintainer at pion@pavelhorak.com.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment asking for a signature; reply on that
pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded in a JSON file on the `cla-signatures` branch of
this repository. It is not sent to any third-party service. You sign once; it
covers all your future contributions.

## If you would rather not

That is a legitimate position and no hard feelings — plenty of people decline
CLAs on principle. Bug reports, reproductions, benchmark results, design
discussion and documentation feedback are all valuable and none of them
require this. If you have a fix you do not want to sign for, describe it in an
issue and it can be implemented independently.
